# Carousel — the marketing day plan

**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6786345170 · **Site:** carrousel-app.netlify.app
**Price:** $5.99 USD / $7.99 CAD, one-time · **iPad only** (iPadOS 26+)

---

## The one goal

Carousel is live but **invisible in search** (0 ratings, a generic name). Nothing
you do Friday changes that directly. What changes it is **reviews + a base of
real users**, which compound into search ranking over weeks. So Friday's single
job is:

> **Convert the people who already like you into installs and honest reviews.**

Everything below serves that. Downloads are nice; the first **30–40 honest
reviews** are the asset. A brand-new paid app with 25 thoughtful 5-star reviews
looks completely different from one with zero — to buyers, to Apple, and to a
Product Hunt/HN audience later.

---

## The strategic call: stage it (read this first)

You *can* cram Product Hunt + Show HN + network + promo codes into one Friday.
You shouldn't. Here's the honest version:

- **Product Hunt and Show HN are one-shot, midweek events.** Both perform best
  Tue–Thu, both demand you sit in the comments all day, and PH's day runs
  midnight–midnight *Pacific* (12:01am PT = 4am your time). You can't do a real
  PH launch, a real HN launch, and a real network push well on the same day.
- **A PH/HN launch with 0 reviews is weak.** The same launch backed by "4.9★,
  40 reviews, here's what people said" converts far better.

So the plan that actually wins:

- **Friday = "founder's day": personal outreach + promo codes + social + niche
  communities + press pitches.** All of this works great on a Friday and doesn't
  depend on an algorithm or a specific hour. This is what *seeds the reviews.*
- **The following Tuesday/Wednesday = Product Hunt, then Show HN the next
  morning** — launched *with* the reviews and a testimonial or two Friday earned
  you. (Kits already written in this folder.)

If you'd rather fire Product Hunt on Friday anyway, jump to "Variant: PH on
Friday" at the bottom. But the staged version is the recommendation.

## Before Friday (do this Thu evening — 45 min)

1. **Generate promo codes.** App Store Connect → your app → in the left sidebar
   under the version, **Promo Codes** (older UI) / **App Store → Promotions →
   Promo Codes**. You get **100 per version**; each is a one-tap redeem link, and
   **they expire 28 days after you generate them** — so generate ~40 now for
   Friday and keep the rest for the PH week. (There's no API for this; it's the
   one ASC thing that's UI-only.) A redeemer gets the paid app free *and can leave
   a review* — which is the whole point.
2. **Build your list** in the tracker (`friday-outreach.csv`, next to this file).
   The hard filter: **do they own an iPad?** A code is wasted on someone who
   doesn't. Bonus points if they own an *idle/old* iPad — that's literally the
   pitch.
3. **Record a 15–20s screen capture of the drift** on a real iPad (photo → quote
   → calendar card cross-fading, plus the day→night shift if you can). This is
   the single highest-value asset you don't have yet — a still can't show "calm,
   drifting." Post it natively on X/LinkedIn; it outperforms every screenshot.
   (I can stitch a rough one from the app screens if you want a placeholder.)
4. **Pre-write nothing from scratch Friday** — the templates are below. Friday is
   for sending, not drafting.

## Promo codes — who, how many, and the ask

**100 total. Suggested split (adjust to your actual contacts):**

| Segment | Codes | Why them |
|---|---|---|
| Close network with an iPad who'll actually *use* it | ~20 | The review seed. Parents, grandparents, friends with kids. Your real target user. |
| Fellow makers / indie-dev friends | ~12 | They'll try it, get the "removed the AI" story, and amplify. |
| Apple-indie press & newsletters | ~15 | A calm, private, well-made iPad app is exactly their beat (list below). |
| Francophone press / creators | ~10 | Your unfair advantage — a French-first app in an underserved niche. |
| Filmmaker / creative friends | ~10 | People with taste who amplify; the golden-hour aesthetic is their language. |
| **Reserve for inbound** | **~30** | "DM me and I'll send a code" is a great reply on HN/PH/X. Keep a stash. |

**How to ask (this matters more than the code):** be specific and low-pressure.
Never "please give me 5 stars." Instead:

> "Here's a free code. If it earns a spot on a shelf in your house, an **honest**
> App Store review is the one thing that helps a brand-new app most — even two
> lines. And if it's not for you, no worries at all."

**Redeeming (tell them, they always ask):** App Store app → tap your photo
top-right → **Redeem Gift Card or Code** → enter it. Or just tap the code link on
the iPad.

## Friday, hour by hour (Atlantic Time)

The order is deliberate: the highest-value, least-scalable work (1:1 messages)
goes first, while you're fresh; broadcast work goes later.

**8:00–10:00 — Personal outreach (the most important block).**
Send the segmented promo-code messages *individually*, with a real personal line
at the top of each. This is 1:1, not a blast — 40 warm notes beat 4,000
impressions. Prioritise the "close network with an iPad" segment: they convert to
reviews at the highest rate. Log each send in the tracker.

**10:00–11:00 — Social posts.**
Post the X thread (EN, then FR a couple hours later) and the LinkedIn post — copy
in `social.md`, already on the paid angle. **Attach the drift video.** Then reply
to every comment for the rest of the day; early replies are what keep a post
moving.

**11:00–12:30 — Niche communities (see list). Tailor each; don't paste.**
Lead with the *story*, not "buy my app." The "I removed the AI to keep it
serverless" and "it never crops a face" angles are the ones that earn comments.

**12:30–1:30 — Lunch + first check-in.** Skim reviews, reply to everything, note
who redeemed.

**1:30–3:30 — Press & newsletter pitches.**
Short, personal emails (template below) with a code attached. One email each,
tailored — a writer can tell a blast. This is a numbers game with a long tail:
most won't reply Friday, some reply next week, one might write it up.

**3:30–4:30 — Francophone push.**
The French-first angle deserves its own block: FR communities, FR creators, and a
note to any francophone tech writer you know. This is where you're not competing
with 10,000 other indie apps.

**4:30–6:00 — Loop back.** Thank everyone who shared or reviewed (publicly where
you can — it prompts others). Answer every comment and DM. Send a few more codes
to anyone who expressed interest.

**Throughout:** respond within minutes, everywhere. Engagement is the one
variable you fully control, and it's what people remember.

## Leverage your network — specific asks (not "please share")

Vague asks get vague results. Match the ask to the person:

- **Friends/family with an iPad →** "Try it on that old iPad in the drawer, and
  if you like it, an honest review helps more than anything." (They *are* the
  target user — this isn't a favour, it's a fit.)
- **People with an audience →** "A repost of my launch post **Friday morning**
  would genuinely help — that's when it counts." (Specific + time-boxed = they
  actually do it.)
- **Fellow makers →** "If it's your kind of thing, a comment on the Show HN /
  Product Hunt next week means a lot." (Save this for the PH/HN day.)
- **Photographer / parent / grandparent friends →** a personal note. They'll get
  it instantly and they talk to other parents and grandparents.
- **Your filmmaker network →** the aesthetic is their language. Even a "this is
  beautiful" quote-post from a visual person you respect is social proof money
  can't buy.

**Your genuine edge — the francophone angle.** A bilingual, French-*first*,
privacy-respecting iPad app is a combination almost no indie app can claim. FR
tech media and communities are far less saturated than English ones. Lean in
hard: it's the one lane where you're not a needle in a stack.

## Where to post (with the norms — breaking them gets you removed)

**English communities:**
- **r/digitalminimalism** — sharpest fit. Calm, no notifications, anti-attention.
  Post as a story ("I built a screen that asks for nothing"), not an ad.
- **r/SideProject**, **r/iOSProgramming**, **Indie Hackers** — maker audience;
  lead with the build story (removed the AI, the PHImageManager crop bug).
- **r/iPad** — real users; check their self-promo rule (often a weekend thread or
  flair required).
- **r/privacy** — only via the *privacy story*, never "buy my app"; they're strict.
- **r/simpleliving, r/minimalism** — adjacent; be a member, not a marketer.
- Skip **r/apple** (no self-promo). **Mastodon** + **Bluesky** Apple/indie-dev
  circles are friendly and worth a post each.

**Apple-indie press & newsletters (a calm private iPad app is squarely their beat):**
- **MacStories** (Federico Viticci) · **Six Colors** (Jason Snell) · **512 Pixels**
  (Stephen Hackett) · **The Sweet Setup** · **9to5Mac** apps desk · **AppAddict**
  · **Indie iOS Focus** and similar indie-app newsletters · **AppStories** /
  **Cool Stuff Found**.
- The hook that gets a reply: *"I removed the only AI feature before launch to keep
  it serverless"* — that's contrarian in 2026 and writers bite on contrarian.

**Francophone:**
- FR indie/tech communities and Discords, francophone Apple creators, and any
  Acadian/NB or Québec tech contact you have. A short FR pitch (template below).

## What to measure (and what to ignore)

- **Track:** reviews (count + what they say), redemptions, and honest
  conversations started. These are the compounding assets.
- **Ignore:** impressions, "reach", vanity upvote counts. For a niche paid app,
  30 real users who love it and review it beats 5,000 people who scrolled past.
- **A humane target for Friday:** 25–40 honest reviews, a dozen genuine users, one
  or two shares from people with taste, and 2–3 press replies (even "maybe later"
  counts). That's a strong day and it sets up the PH/HN week.

## After Friday

1. Reviews accrue → search ranking slowly improves. Check weekly.
2. **The following Tue/Wed: Product Hunt, then Show HN** — now with reviews and a
   testimonial to point at. Kits are ready in this folder.
3. Fold better **keywords** ("photo frame", "slideshow", "digital picture frame")
   into a **1.0.1** — keywords are frozen until the next version, and those are the
   terms people actually search for a thing like this.
4. Keep replying to reviews in App Store Connect. It's visible and it's rare.

## Variant: Product Hunt on Friday (if you insist)

- Schedule the PH post for **12:01am PT** (PH lets you schedule; you don't have to
  be awake). Post the maker's comment the moment it goes live.
- Then the whole Friday hour-by-hour above becomes *PH-support*: your outreach and
  social all point at the PH page for the day, and you live in the PH comments.
- **Drop the Show HN to the following week** regardless — you cannot do PH and HN
  in the same day and do either justice.
- Downside you're accepting: launching PH before Friday's reviews land, and on a
  weaker PH day. Your call.
